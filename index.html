Below the CMMC conditional threshold

You'd need 88 or higher to earn a conditional Level 2 status with a POA&M. The gap is usually a handful of 3- and 5-point controls: MFA, FIPS-validated encryption, logging, and incident response.

DoD Assessment Methodology: every supplier starts at 110 and loses 1, 3 or 5 points for each requirement not met. Conditional CMMC Level 2 status requires at least 88 points, with remaining items closed within 180 days.

CMMC questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Do we need CMMC Level 1 or Level 2?

If you only handle Federal Contract Information, Level 1 (15 basic safeguards, annual self-assessment) usually applies. If you receive or create Controlled Unclassified Information, such as drawings, specs or technical data marked CUI, you're in Level 2 territory and need all 110 NIST SP 800-171 requirements. Scoping that correctly is the first thing we check.

What is an SPRS score and who sees it?

It's your self-assessed NIST SP 800-171 score, from −203 to 110, posted in DoD's Supplier Performance Risk System. Contracting officers and primes can see it when they evaluate you. It has to reflect your real controls, because an inflated score creates legal risk.

Can we pass with legacy machines we can't upgrade?

Usually, yes. Unsupported controllers and CNC workstations can often be segmented out of the CUI boundary or covered by documented compensating controls. That's how the AdvancedPCB program handled equipment leadership chose to keep.

Do we have to move to Microsoft GCC High?

Not always. GCC High is a common way to build a CUI enclave, but the right answer depends on how much CUI you handle, who needs it, and your export-control obligations. We'll compare enclave and full-tenant options with cost before you commit.

Can you work with our IT provider or MSP?

Yes, and we prefer to. Your MSP keeps running day-to-day IT; we set the security program, define what evidence is needed, and verify the work holds up to an assessor.

How long does it take to get ready?

A Readiness Check takes two to three weeks. Reaching assessment-ready Level 2 typically takes several months, depending on your starting score, sites and budget. You'll get a dated roadmap at the end of the Readiness Check.
